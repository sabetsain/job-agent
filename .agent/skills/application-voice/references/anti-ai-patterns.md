# Anti-AI Patterns for CVs & Cover Letters

Reference checklist adapted from `robertguss/claude-code-toolkit` specifically for job application documents (English & Danish). Run every reworked or drafted CV and Cover Letter through this filter before delivering.

## 1. Cover Letter Boilerplate Openers & Closers
**Strip out:**
- *"I am writing to apply for the [Role] position at [Company]..."*
- *"I am writing to express my keen/enthusiastic interest in..."*
- *"Please accept this letter as my application for..."*
- *"Thank you for your time and consideration. I look forward to the opportunity to contribute to your esteemed organization."*
- Danish equivalents: *"Jeg skriver hermed for at søge stillingen som..."*, *"Det er med stor interesse, at jeg har læst jeres stillingsopslag..."*

**Replace with:** Sebastian's direct hook tied to the company's actual product/mission, and his warm, plain closing (`"I am excited by the opportunity to further discuss how my background fits the role!"` / `"Jeg ser meget frem til muligheden for at uddybe, hvordan min baggrund matcher jeres behov!"`).

## 2. Em-Dash & Structural Tells
- **Zero em-dashes (`—`)** in cover letters. Rewrite em-dash interruptions as clean separate sentences, commas, or (sparingly) a semicolon.
- **No mechanical rule-of-three padding** (*"passion, dedication, and technical excellence"*). List real items in whatever count is true.
- **No false ranges** (*"from backend architecture to fostering a culture of innovation"*).
- **No empty negative parallelisms** (*"It is not just about writing code; it is about empowering teams"* — only use `"not only... but also"` when contrasting two concrete, distinct facts).

## 3. Superficial `-ing` Significance Trailers
AI loves bolting a shallow participle onto the end of a concrete sentence:
- *"...ensuring seamless scalability and optimal performance."*
- *"...highlighting my commitment to engineering excellence."*
- *"...showcasing my ability to collaborate across cross-functional teams."*
- *"...aligning seamlessly with your team's mission."*
- Danish equivalents: *"...hvilket understreger min evne til..."*, *"...og dermed bidrage til..."* (when vague).

**Fix:** Cut the trailer completely, or replace it with a real, verifiable outcome.

## 4. Significance Puffery & Corporate Buzzwords
**English words to cut or replace with plain verbs/nouns:**
- `delve into`, `navigate the complexities`, `landscape`, `tapestry`, `multifaceted`, `intricate`
- `stands as a testament`, `plays a vital/pivotal/crucial role`, `underscores`, `foster`, `embark on a journey`
- `spearheaded`, `orchestrated`, `pioneered`, `synergized`, `championed`, `utilize` (→ `use`)
- `cutting-edge`, `world-class`, `robust`, `comprehensive`, `dynamic`, `fast-paced`, `results-driven`, `self-starter`, `hit the ground running`

**Danish equivalents to watch for:**
- `navigere i kompleksiteten`, `spiller en afgørende rolle`, `står som et vidnesbyrd`, `banebrydende`, `dynamisk miljø`, `brænder for at skabe værdi`, `proaktiv holdspiller`, `synergi`

## 5. Vague Hedging & Unsubstantiated Quantifiers
- Avoid empty placeholders: `various`, `numerous`, `several`, `significant`, `extensive`, `diverse` (and Danish `diverse`, `adskillige`, `betydelig`) when a real tool, project, or approximate count (`~300`, `~1200`) is available in the source.
- Never invent a number to fix vagueness — if no number exists in the source, state the concrete work cleanly without a fake metric.

## 6. Final Quick-Pass Audit
Before delivering any reworked or drafted document, verify:
- [ ] Zero em-dashes (`—`) in cover letters
- [ ] Zero contractions in cover letters
- [ ] No boilerplate opener (*"I am writing to apply..."*)
- [ ] No superficial `-ing` significance trailers
- [ ] No corporate/AI buzzwords from Section 4
- [ ] Every tool, metric, title, and experience traces directly to the source input / Master Profile
- [ ] Correct sign-off (`Kind Regards,` for EN / `Med venlig hilsen,` for DA) and at most one `!`
